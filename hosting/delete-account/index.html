Privacy Policy

Delete your SkiManiacs account

You can delete your account at any time:

  1. Open the SkiManiacs app and log in.
  2. Open the menu and choose My Account.
  3. Choose Delete my account and confirm.

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile (username, name, email) and your saved favorites from our database.

If you cannot access the app, email info@skimanijaci.com from the address registered on your account and we will delete it for you.

Brisanje naloga SkiManiacs

Nalog možete obrisati u svakom trenutku:

  1. Otvorite aplikaciju SkiManiacs i prijavite se.
  2. U meniju izaberite Moj nalog.
  3. Izaberite Obriši moj nalog i potvrdite.

Brisanjem naloga trajno se uklanjaju vaš profil (korisničko ime, ime, email) i sačuvane omiljene ponude iz naše baze.

Ako nemate pristup aplikaciji, pošaljite email na info@skimanijaci.com sa adrese registrovane na nalogu i obrisaćemo ga.
